## Presentations

- Students should present **7 fairly recent papers** that are considered to be either on the "frontier" in labor economics or "classics" that I do not have time to cover, but that complement well the material covered in class. **Each presentation will last 1 hour and 20 minutes.**
- Students must acquire a critical and objective perspective on the paper being presented. What are the strengths? What are the weaknesses? What is the contribution? Is there room for improvement? Top presentations are the ones that are particularly successful at linking the results of the paper with the material covered in class.
- I will be very proactive during your presentations. You should prepare to be challenged and asked difficult questions on the spot. That's just to prepare you for what's coming next in your career.
- Students should also be engaged during the presentation and ask questions they believe are relevant, just like in a real seminar.



### How papers get assigned

- First you need to form a team of two. To claim a paper, **email me** saying which paper you would like to present and **why** — specifically, how the paper connects to the research interests of the team. Be concrete.
- If you do not send an email, or if several students ask for the same paper, **the remaining papers will be assigned at random by Claude**.
- Final assignments will be posted in the schedule table above on the class Github website.
